🗑️ Delete Your RideShare Account

We're sorry to see you go! You can delete your RideShare account and all associated data directly from the app.

� Delete Your Account In-App (Recommended)

Follow these steps to delete your account directly in the app:

  • 1️⃣ Open the RideShare app and log in
  • 2️⃣ Go to the Profile tab (bottom navigation)
  • 3️⃣ Scroll down and tap "Delete Account"
  • 4️⃣ Confirm your decision when prompted
  • 5️⃣ Your account and all data will be permanently deleted immediately

⚠️ Important Information

  • Permanent deletion: This action cannot be undone
  • Immediate effect: Your account is deleted instantly when you confirm in the app
  • Data removal: All your personal data, rides, profile photo, and authentication data will be permanently deleted

🔒 What Data Will Be Deleted

  • Your profile information (name, email, photo)
  • All rides you created
  • Your participation in other rides
  • Your profile photo (if uploaded)
  • Authentication data (Firebase account)
  • Push notification tokens
