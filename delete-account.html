🗑️ Delete Your RideShare Account

We're sorry to see you go! If you need to delete your RideShare account and all associated data, we're here to help.

📋 How to Request Account Deletion

Send an email to the address above with the following information:

  • 📧 Your registered email address (the one you used to sign up)
  • 👤 Your display name in the app (if you set one)
  • 📅 Approximate date when you created your account
  • 🆔 Any additional details to help us identify your account

⚠️ Important Information

  • Permanent deletion: This action cannot be undone
  • Data removal: All your personal data, rides, and messages will be permanently deleted
  • Processing time: Account deletion requests are processed within 7 business days
  • Confirmation: You will receive a confirmation email once your account is deleted

🔒 What Data Will Be Deleted

  • Your profile information (name, email, photo)
  • All rides you created or joined
  • Your ride history and messages
  • Any stored preferences or settings
  • Authentication data

💬 Alternative: Contact Support

If you're experiencing issues with the app or have concerns, you can also contact us for support before deciding to delete your account. We're here to help!

📱 Immediate Account Deactivation

If you need immediate account deactivation while waiting for permanent deletion, you can:

  • Log out of the app on all devices
  • Uninstall the RideShare app
  • Your account will remain inactive until deletion is processed
