Privacy Policy for RideShare

Last updated: January 2025

1. Introduction

RideShare ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Personal Information

  • Email address (for account creation and authentication)
  • Display name and first name
  • Nationality
  • WhatsApp number (for ride coordination)
  • Profile photo (optional)

2.2 Ride Information

  • Departure and arrival cities
  • Travel dates and times
  • Messages and ride descriptions
  • Transport mode preferences

2.3 Technical Information

  • Device information and app usage analytics
  • Crash reports and performance data
  • Push notification tokens

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and maintain our ride-sharing service
  • To facilitate connections between travelers
  • To send notifications about ride updates
  • To improve our app performance and user experience
  • To ensure platform safety and security

4. Information Sharing

We share your information only in the following circumstances:

  • With other users when you create or join a ride (name, photo, contact info)
  • With Firebase/Google for app functionality and analytics
  • When required by law or to protect our rights

5. Data Security

We use Firebase Authentication and Firestore to secure your data with industry-standard encryption and security measures.

6. Your Rights

  • Access your personal data
  • Update or correct your information
  • Delete your account and associated data
  • Opt-out of non-essential communications

7. Third-Party Services

Our app uses the following third-party services:

  • Firebase (Google) - Authentication, database, analytics
  • WhatsApp - External communication (user-initiated)

8. Children's Privacy

Our service is not intended for users under 16 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 16.

9. International Data Transfers

Your data may be processed in countries other than your own, including the United States, where Firebase servers are located.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new policy in the app.
